Why this direction: The Madaglasht-side ascent is more gradual, which is why trekkers generally approach from there; the descent into Lohigal Gol on the north side is notably steeper and more difficult — factor that asymmetry into planning which way you'd want to go.\n\nDays/cost: About 12 days total including Islamabad-Chitral travel on both ends, roughly US$1,400 all-inclusive on a guided departure (roughly PKR 389,000); summer (June onward) is the recommended season.\n\nConnects to: this sits in the same broader Golen Gol system as the Golen–Bashkargol–Kumrat traverse covered in Region 6 — if Chitral's remote valley-hopping routes interest you, these two treks share territory and could plausibly be combined or compared when planning.\n\nRegion 7 is now fully covered.
